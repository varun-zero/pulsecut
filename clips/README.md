## Why quality dropped before (and what was fixed)

The original clips looked low-quality because of two compounding settings:

1. **Source download** — `vod.rs` used yt-dlp `-f worst[ext=mp4][height>=360]/worst`,
   which fetched the **lowest-resolution** stream (~360p, ~200 kbps video). The
   renderer then upscaled that to the 1080×1920 vertical canvas — sharpening a
   blurry source cannot recover detail.
2. **Encode** — `libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 18`, AAC at 160 kbps.

Fixes applied in this commit:

- **Download**: `bestvideo[height<=1080]+bestaudio` merged to MP4 (1080p is the
  highest source available and matches the render canvas). Override with
  `PULSECUT_SOURCE_FORMAT`. The downloaded source is now **1920×1080 @ ~3.8 Mbps**.
- **Encode**: `-preset slow`, CRF 16, AAC 256 kbps (x264); NVENC upgraded to
  `p7`/CQ17 constant-quality, QSV to `slow`/global-quality.
- Source section length unchanged at 180 s (`PULSECUT_MAX_SOURCE_SECONDS`).
